# Svaki sat je važan / Every Hour Matters

**SR** — Interaktivni retrospektivni prikaz rezultata rada *„Svaki sat je važan: satna prostorno-vremenska procena kvaliteta vazduha, dominantnih polutanata i potencijalnog opterećenja stanovništva u Beogradu (2024–2025)“*: satna EAQI analiza na 27 automatskih mernih stanica (AMS) lokalne mreže Gradskog zavoda za javno zdravlje Beograd (GZJZ).

**EN** — Interactive retrospective presentation of the results of the study *“Every Hour Matters: An Hourly Spatio-Temporal Assessment of Air Quality, Dominant Pollutants, and Potential Population Burden in Belgrade, Serbia (2024–2025)”*: hourly EAQI analysis at 27 automatic monitoring stations (AMS) of the local network of the Belgrade City Institute of Public Health (GZJZ).

- Aplikacija (srpski): `index.html`
- Application (English): `index_en.html`

Prikaz je retrospektivan (2024–2025) i ne prikazuje trenutno stanje. Za trenutni kvalitet vazduha: https://vazduh.sepa.gov.rs/
The presentation is retrospective (2024–2025) and does not show current conditions. For current air quality: https://vazduh.sepa.gov.rs/

Satni podaci o kvalitetu vazduha vlasništvo su Gradskog zavoda za javno zdravlje Beograd i prikazuju se uz njegovu saglasnost. Stanovništvo: WorldPop R2025A (Bondarenko et al., 2025, https://doi.org/10.5258/SOTON/WP00839).

Hourly air quality data are the property of the Belgrade City Institute of Public Health and are presented with its consent. Population: WorldPop R2025A (Bondarenko et al., 2025, https://doi.org/10.5258/SOTON/WP00839).
